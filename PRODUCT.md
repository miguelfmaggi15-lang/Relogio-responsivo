# Oyster Atelier

Local interactive study of the black-dial silver Rolex Oyster Perpetual. The user requested a complete visual and interaction redesign with real multi-angle references and explicitly delegated the composition after clarifying that unrestricted exploration, including focusing on any exploded component, is the primary purpose.

The current approved concept is an interactive atelier: cinematic introduction, freely orbitable and pannable 3D model, seven selectable components, independent movement, three-axis positioning, continuous axial separation, focus/isolation, camera/lighting presets and a photographic reference gallery. This supersedes the former requirement to preserve the Aurel page composition. The supplied lower Claude/Opus video remains motion inspiration, not an exact source or engineering model.

The subject remains a silver Oyster Perpetual with black dial. No commerce, accounts, external API keys or paid rendering services. No original CAD was supplied: the 3D model and internal mechanism are artistic reconstructions, with explicit disclosure. Real source photographs are distinguished from the 3D mechanism. Images belong to their source authors, with source links in the gallery and embedded origin metadata.

Implementation: Vite, vanilla JavaScript, Three.js and OrbitControls. Local workspace only. Mouse, keyboard and touch input are supported; no autoplay is forced, camera transitions respect reduced motion, and the source photograph/gallery remain available when WebGL fails. Physical touch hardware has not been used for validation.

The original video and prior implementation snapshots remain under references/. Current design contract: .impeccable/surfaces/atelier.md. New styling is documented in DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json. Original user-supplied files outside the project remain read-only.
